SPEED: 425 -> 900 WPM

An RSVP speed reader for Windows — one word at a time, the red pivot letter pinned where your eye already is — built around the two things reading science holds against every other word-streamer: it restores regression (Schotter, Tran & Rayner 2014) with a one-key recovery loop, and it verifies comprehension with a local-AI quiz whose every question must prove itself with a verbatim quote from your text. Dark-only, contract-first, every pixel its own.

the intervention ladder

Left — replay

A lapse. This sentence again, at pace. No ceremony.

hold — BRAKE

A wobble. Speed glides to a crawl, the fisheye ribbon flows along; drag left to scrub back; release to ease home.

Space — recover

Comprehension lost. A readable page; click the word you last truly read; resume there, slow, climbing.

Esc — seal

The sitting ends honestly: speeds, rewinds, skips and quiz score into the ledger.

what ships

The deck

Eleven drawn VCR buttons: start-over with a fresh ramp, paragraph and section jumps, play, replay, stop, the brake pedal, speed, cloud.

Scholarly timing

Hebrew and Greek linger and never wear a fake pivot; transliterations, refs and acronyms carry their own beats; optional foreign-script skip.

The word cloud

Ribbon words warm by significance; W (or the edge tab) opens the Document / Section / Paragraph cloud.

The reading window

Keys 1–9: the classic lone word, or up to ten — real neighbors flank the primary, fisheye-small, word-cloud-inked, dimmer with distance. The red pivot never moves.

Quiz before reading

A local model writes questions at load time, in a worker process. Verbatim-quote gate; SQ3R preview; self-judged score into the ledger.

Any text in

Paste, a drive-hopping dialog, Explorer drag-and-drop anywhere, PDFs through bundled Poppler. UTF-8 clean.

The honest ledger

Dialed peak vs achieved WPM vs retention, every sitting. Crash-safe bookmarks; continue where you left off.
