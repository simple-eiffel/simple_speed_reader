SPEED: 595 WPM
  • Recovery loop — one key out of the stream, click your last-comprehended word, re-ramp from 250 WPM. Regression restored (Schotter et al. 2014).
  • Quiz before reading — a local model writes a quote-verified quiz from your text at load time; preview it (SQ3R), take it after, log the score.
  • Three postures — running is only the stream; paused drawers the controls over a readable context page; stopped opens the full cockpit.
  • Scholar timing — Hebrew/Greek linger with no fake pivot, transliteration and acronym classes, optional foreign-script skip.
  • Honest log — dialed WPM vs achieved WPM vs retention, in SQLite, local only.
